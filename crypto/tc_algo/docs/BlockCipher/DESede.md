The OIDs are OIW's DESede, which means ECB with PKCS#7 padding, and RSA's DES-EDE3-CBC, which means
CBC with PKCS#7 padding.

P1: The modes with an IV accept `KeyWithIvOwned`, `ParamsWithIvOwned<KeyOwned>`, or
`ParamsWithIvOwned` holding a `Box<dyn Params>` or `Arc<dyn Params>` containing `KeyOwned`. `DESEDE`
and `DESEDE3` generate a `KeyOwned`. The `Box` goes with one message. An `Arc` lets one key serve
many messages, each with a fresh IV, without copying the key.
