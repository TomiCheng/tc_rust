# Camellia

The `camellia` feature has the rows. A block of 16 bytes and a key of 16, 24 or 32, as AES; a
Japanese and ISO standard.

The OIDs are NTT's for 128-, 192- and 256-bit keys, and mean CBC with PKCS#7 padding.

P1: The modes with an IV accept `KeyWithIvOwned`, `ParamsWithIvOwned<KeyOwned>`, or
`ParamsWithIvOwned` holding a `Box<dyn Params>` or `Arc<dyn Params>` containing `KeyOwned`.
`CAMELLIA`, `CAMELLIA128`, `CAMELLIA192`, and `CAMELLIA256` generate a `KeyOwned`. The `Box` goes
with one message. An `Arc` lets one key serve many messages, each with a fresh IV, without copying
the key.
