The OIDs are NIST's for AES-128, AES-192 and AES-256 in each mode; as in Bouncy Castle, those of ECB
and CBC mean PKCS#7 padding. All three find the same row, so the length of the key comes from the
params.

The AEAD modes, `AES/GCM/NOPADDING`, `AES/CCM/NOPADDING`, `AES/EAX/NOPADDING` and
`AES/OCB/NOPADDING`, are on [AEAD ciphers](../AeadCipher.md), with those of the other algorithms.

P1: The modes with an IV take the key and the IV in any of four ways: a `KeyWithIvOwned` of their
bytes; a `ParamsWithIvOwned<KeyOwned>`; or a `ParamsWithIvOwned` of a `Box<dyn Params>` or an
`Arc<dyn Params>` holding a `KeyOwned`, as `AES128`, `AES192` and `AES256` generate it. The `Box`
goes with one message; the `Arc` lets one key serve many messages, each with a new IV, without
copying the key.
